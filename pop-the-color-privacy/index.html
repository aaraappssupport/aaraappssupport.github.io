Pop The Color

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026 · App version 1.0.0 · Android package and iOS bundle ID com.aaraapps.popthecolor · Publisher: AARA Apps

Pop The Color ("the app") is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not request internet access and has no network code. It does not collect or send any information to the developer or to anyone else. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services, no social features and no accounts or sign-in. It does not use the advertising ID, your location, your contacts, your photos, your camera, your microphone or any other device sensor.

Information stored only on your device

  • Game progress and records: unlocked levels, stars, best move counts and best times, and per-level play statistics (attempts, completions, play time and moves played) shown in My Records.
  • Player name and player ID: a default name ("Player") and a random anonymous ID created on first launch. They are stored locally only and are not sent anywhere by this version.

This information is kept in the app's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app (or, on Android, clear its data). On Android it is not included in cloud backups. On iPhone it is included in your own iCloud or computer backup like other app data; the developer has no access to it.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions and no iPhone permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's store listing will describe what is shared.
