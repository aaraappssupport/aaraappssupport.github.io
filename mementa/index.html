About the app

Mementa turns the things you mean to remember into reminders in a few taps. Pick a template for medicine, bills, renewals or travel, set the date and time, and save. Your reminders, notes and history stay on your device.

Common questions

A reminder didn’t reach me

Open More → Reminder reliability in Mementa. It checks that notifications, alerts and sounds are on, takes you to Settings if one is off, and can send a test reminder in 60 seconds. Also check that a Focus mode isn’t silencing Mementa.

How do I delete my reminders?

Delete a reminder inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your iPhone. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, that backup may still hold a copy until you remove the backup.

What does the weather card send?

The weather card is optional and stays off until you choose a city. The city you search for and its rounded coordinates are sent to the free Open-Meteo service to fetch the forecast. Nothing else leaves your device. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. Mementa has no account, no ads and no analytics. Read the full privacy policy.
