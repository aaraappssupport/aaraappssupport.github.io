Pop The Color

Plan your pops. Collect the color.

Pop The Color is a free strategy puzzle you can play fully offline. Tap a group of two or more touching tiles of the same color to pop it, collect the target color or shape the board before your moves run out, and earn 3 stars by matching the shortest solution — across 100 solver-checked levels in 10 worlds, with optional online ranks. No ads, no purchases, no account.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Uninstalling the game (or clearing its data on Android) erases it. On iPhone, your device's iCloud or computer backup includes it like other app data. Online ranks, if you turn them on, receive your level results but cannot restore your progress on another phone.

Is every level possible?

Yes. Every board is fixed and was checked by a solver; 3 stars means you matched the shortest solution. If you're stuck, restart: a level always plays the same way.

Where can I see my best results?

Open My Records from the title screen: best stars and best moves for every level you have completed, plus totals. Tap a level card in the level list for its best time.

How do online ranks work?

They are optional and off until you turn them on (the game asks the first time you open a ranks view; you can change it any time in Settings). When they are on, your level results are shown with your generated name, for example "Snowy Panda 61", ranked by fewest moves, then fastest time. Details: privacy policy.

How do I delete my leaderboard data?

In the game: Settings → Delete my leaderboard data. It removes your results, your name and your anonymous player ID from online ranks and turns them off; your progress on the device stays. Turning online ranks off only stops sending. If you no longer have the game installed, your old entries are anonymous and cannot be traced back to you.
