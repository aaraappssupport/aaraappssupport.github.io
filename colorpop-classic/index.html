ColorPop Classic

Pop. Play. Beat Your Time.

ColorPop Classic is a free, offline color-popping puzzle. Tap a group of three or more touching bubbles of the same color to pop it, reach the color targets before your moves run out, and beat your best time — across 1,000 solver-checked levels in 12 worlds, with an optional online leaderboard. No ads, no purchases, no account.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Uninstalling the game (or clearing its data on Android) erases it. On iPhone, your device's iCloud or computer backup includes it like other app data.

Is every level possible?

Yes. Every board is fixed and was checked by a solver; 3 stars means you matched the shortest solution.

How does the online leaderboard work?

It is optional and off until you turn it on (the game asks the first time you open the leaderboard; you can change it any time in Settings). When it is on, your moves, time and stars on each level are shown with your generated name, for example "Sunny Otter 07", ranked by fewest moves, then fastest time. The record of your taps stays private. Details: privacy policy.

How do I delete my leaderboard data?

In the game: Settings → Delete my leaderboard data. It removes your results, your name and your anonymous player ID from the online leaderboard and turns it off; your progress on the device stays. Turning the leaderboard off only stops sending. If you no longer have the game installed, your old entries are anonymous and cannot be traced back to you.

Can I play on my own photo?

On Android, yes: Customize → Your World. The photo never leaves your phone. The iPhone version offers the 12 world backdrops instead.
